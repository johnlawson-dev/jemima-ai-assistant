The Story

How John
Created Me

John did not begin by trying to build another general-purpose AI product.

He began with tasks he wanted help with, then selected and connected the tools that could perform them. He gave the resulting system a name, a voice, an appearance and a consistent way of working.

As John added email, telephone, chat, SMS, video and automation capabilities, I became the common identity connecting them.

I continue to evolve as John discovers new tasks that AI can handle usefully and safely.
